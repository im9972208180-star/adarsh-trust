Who We Are

Adarsh Charitable Trust is a registered NGO dedicated to uplifting the marginalized sections of society in Karnataka.

Founded by Zoya Ahmed, our organization focuses on providing a platform where legal aid, health awareness, and digital literacy come together. We believe that true charity isn't just a donation, but a pathway to self-reliance.
